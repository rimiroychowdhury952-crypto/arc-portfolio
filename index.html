About me

Learning, designing and building

I'm a B.Com student who enjoys building things for the web. I'm learning web design and development step by step, and I like turning a visual idea into a page that works well on any screen.

My foundation is HTML, CSS and Bootstrap, with basic JavaScript, PHP and MySQL. I also use Photoshop for visual design, and I have learned the basics of SEO and social media marketing. I use AI tools to plan, prototype and get coding support, and I'm still learning how to get the best from them.

I'm early in my journey. I'm looking for freelance projects and internships where I can keep learning while delivering useful work.
